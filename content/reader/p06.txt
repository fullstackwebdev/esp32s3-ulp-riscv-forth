The store is 128 blocks of 128 bytes, sixteen kilobytes,
split by an allocation table rather than by need. A
descriptor is durable and costs blocks of its own; the
source of a phase is transient, needed only while the
upload is compiling it, so it does not have to be paid for
twice. Seeding a line blanks its block with spaces first,
so a short line is padded to the screen width by the store
itself. A flush is what makes any of it survive a reset.
