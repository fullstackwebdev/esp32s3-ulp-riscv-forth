LOAD does not interpret a block by itself. It fetches the
block into the window and arms the interpreter's source
switch, and the next token comes from the block instead of
the console line. One pass of that buffer delivers 95
bytes, so a block is cut at 92 and a cut is legal only
where the machine stands between definitions: never inside
a colon body, never after a colon or a CREATE that is
still waiting for the name it will store.
