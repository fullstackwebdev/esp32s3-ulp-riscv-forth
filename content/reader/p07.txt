This screen is the arithmetic described on the pages
before it. The top line is page times eight plus the line
within the page, and the line at the top of the text is
fetched as block eighty plus that number. j and k move the
top line by one, clamped so the window never runs off the
end; m writes that line into a registry cell; g reads the
cell back and restores both the page and the line.
Nothing here is compiled in except the keys and the bar.
