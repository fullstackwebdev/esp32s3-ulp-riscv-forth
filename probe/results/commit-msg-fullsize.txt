lora: FULL-SIZE packets work (221 B byte-exact), and the phase-chain premise is measured to its limit

tests/lora_fullsize.py, 9/9: 221 B on the air = 16 B Meshtastic header + 5 B protobuf prefix (08 80 02 12 c8) +
a 200-byte body, TxDone 0x0001 on the sender, RxDone 0x0016 on the peer, every byte read back out of the peer's
own RX buffer identical. SETLEN takes the payload length at run time, so the frame is no longer fixed at 26 B;
LoRa's 255 B ceiling is now a caller's number, not a code limit.

PHASE-CHAIN PREMISE (tests/lora_scratch.py). A chain needs memory that survives a dictionary swap, and the only
candidate is the SX1262's own 256-byte buffer. Measured: writing a 16-byte pattern to offset 240 and reading it
back in the same dictionary is exact (11 22 33 ... f0 0f); after an ESP32 reset the bytes are gone. A reset is
harsher than an adopt (it drives the pads), so the adopt case is UNPROVEN, not disproven - proving it needs two
real phases taken through the store, which is the next step. Said plainly rather than assumed either way.

LEVERS PRICED AND REJECTED (docs/31 Appendix H):
- -Os on the ULP build: it was ALREADY -Os (IDF's flags). Appending another bought 0 B - same .text, same
  vm_enter 2104, same pool span 7937. Reverted, because a flag nobody measured is a claim.
- removing unused primitives: eight of the 27 corpus-unused ones (GPIO@ GPIO! ADC@ WAKE PUMP EV@ BRT U/MOD)
  price at 7937 -> 7865 = -72 B, about 9 B each. The cipher needs 329 B, so this lever needs ~37 primitives that
  do not exist. Kept under variants/nohw as the measurement.

Chain cost from sizes already measured: key schedule 825 B of the 999 B window; SubBytes+ShiftRows ~490 B;
MixColumns+AddRoundKey ~430 B; 22 hops per 16-byte block at 4.5 ms/adopt ~= 100 ms, and a 221-byte frame ~1.4 s
against ~2.5 s of air time - affordable, which is why the design is worth building.

Test bugs I hit and fixed, all mine: a one-byte reader that hard-coded its offset and reported the buffer's
leftovers; a 76 B typed write line dropped silently by the ring (R21 again); lorainit+loratx both declare TBL
so they cannot share a dictionary (they are separate phases - the peer defines SETLEN itself); and a protobuf
prefix counted as four bytes when it is five.
